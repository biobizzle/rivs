# 3D Asset Provenance

The following original furniture models are from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released under CC0 1.0. The RIVS scene's architectural space, animation, motion controls, editorial interface, procedural materials, art and lamp were authored specifically for this project.

| Model | Author | License | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sofa 03 | Fran Calvente | CC0 1.0 | https://polyhaven.com/a/sofa_03 |
| Modern Arm Chair 01 | Vibrant Nordic | CC0 1.0 | https://polyhaven.com/a/modern_arm_chair_01 |
| Modern Coffee Table 01 | Amin | CC0 1.0 | https://polyhaven.com/a/modern_coffee_table_01 |
| Potted Plant 02 | Rico Cilliers | CC0 1.0 | https://polyhaven.com/a/potted_plant_02 |

Assets are fetched on the build server through Poly Haven's public API, and self-hosted. The script sends the required unique User-Agent, verifies file lengths and MD5 hashes where published, and blocks a build when essential models cannot be verified.

The user-supplied portrait video references are *not bundled or displayed*. No third-party video logos are used.
